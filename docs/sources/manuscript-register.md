# Manuscript source candidates

Checked through official institutional/project pages on 8 November 2026. These are admission candidates, not installed connectors or an assertion of unrestricted reuse. No manuscript scans are bundled in this repository. A successful catalog-page read does not verify image-service integration, every image's availability, or passage alignment.

| Collection / candidate | Accountable source | Evidence and admission status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected Torah/Hebrew-scripture witnesses among the Dead Sea Scrolls | [Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library, Israel Antiquities Authority](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/) | Official IAA library. Select actual Torah fragments by catalog content; do not label every scroll as Torah. [Terms](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/terms?locale=en_US) reserve reproduction/display/distribution rights with a private-copy exception. Start catalog/link-only until the proposed viewing use is admitted; no bulk redistribution assumed. |
| Greek Bible, Codex Sinaiticus | [Official project electronic-edition description](https://codexsinaiticus.org/en/project/edition.aspx), [scholarly transcription](https://codexsinaiticus.org/en/project/transcription.aspx) | Describes page images, linked transcription/corrections, physical descriptions, and selected translations. Rights and current per-image availability need admission. Live direct access timed out during this check; indexed official documentation was readable. Do not claim a working connector. |
| Greek Bible, Vat.gr.1209 | [DigiVatLib, Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Vat.gr.1209/0050?ling=en) | Official catalog/viewer lists shelfmark, fourth-century catalog date, image copyright attribution, and an [IIIF manifest](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiif/MSS_Vat.gr.1209/manifest.json). Manifest access does not itself authorize reuse. Exact passage/canvas mapping and current access remain to be tested. |
| Early Quran manuscripts across collections | [Corpus Coranicum manuscript overview](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts) | Scholarly catalog with manuscript metadata, images and some transliterations; its overview explicitly notes unavailable images where rights are missing. The current project credits maintenance/publication since 2025 to Corpus Coranicum e.V., following the BBAW project period. Admit the actual holding collection, record, image rights and transcription separately. The current page is JavaScript-dependent; no connector tested. |
| Quran, Birmingham manuscript | [University of Birmingham / Cadbury Research Library](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran) | Official collection. [Free-access image page](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/free-access-images) delegates images to the library's Flickr and limits downloads to private study; publication requests go to imaging services. Register only the institution-linked assets, not arbitrary Flickr uploads. |

Each admitted record needs stable institution/collection identity, verified official domains and delegated hosts, catalog URL, shelfmark, work/fragment coverage, folio/canvas IDs, image/transcription source, attribution, rights by use (view/cache/export/external processing/training), access status, reviewed date, revision/hash where available, and provenance for any passage/region alignment. Metadata and transcription can have different terms from images.

Record scholarly dating as an attributed range with its method and uncertainty. For example, Birmingham's [official FAQ](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/faqs) distinguishes radiocarbon dating of the parchment from the ink and writing. Do not display a material-date interval as an exact date of composition or a proof of direct prophetic authorship.

The manuscript browser accepts candidates only from this reviewed institutional/scholarly registry. Domain matching alone is insufficient: assess editorial responsibility, object provenance, catalog identity and the specific record. Unknown mirrors, unsourced reconstructions, generated images and popular polemics are not manuscript evidence. Academic disagreements remain attributed; an official host does not make every historical interpretation certain.

Hadith and tafsir manuscript packs are not admitted. Quran manuscript inclusion does not automatically admit a website's unrelated commentary or hadith. Follow the [primary-text and context-only policy](../plans/research-method-and-model-architecture.md) before any secondary material can enter research evidence.
