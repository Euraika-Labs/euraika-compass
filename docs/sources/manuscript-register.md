# Manuscript sources for personal research

Starting references checked through institutional/project pages on 8 November 2026. This is an extensible research register, not an exclusive list or an approval board. Compass is for personal, neutral theological research: authentic, traceable online material can be used even when its host is not an institution. No manuscript scans are bundled in this repository. A catalog-page read does not verify image-service integration, every image's availability, or passage alignment.

The user can add a source encountered online. Record the identified owner/author/project or holding collection, source and original catalog links where available, shelfmark/object identity, work/fragment coverage, folio/canvas IDs, image/transcription origin and attribution. Compare copied images with a catalog or another traceable reference when possible. Preserve unknown fields instead of inventing them. A verified personal repository, project site or authentic copy is usable; institutional affiliation is not mandatory.

Keep actual access conditions and relevant use notes with the record: personal viewing, local reference copy, external model processing, sharing and training are different uses. Unknown permission for training or public distribution does not block ordinary personal reading. Known restrictions affect the relevant operation; use the source viewer or a link when copying/embedding is unavailable. No access-control bypass or automatic redistribution follows from finding a source online. Metadata and transcription can have different conditions from images. Store the access date and revision/hash where available, and evidence for any passage/region alignment.

Record scholarly dating as an attributed range with its method and uncertainty. For example, Birmingham's [official FAQ](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/faqs) distinguishes radiocarbon dating of the parchment from the ink and writing. Do not display a material-date interval as an exact date of composition or a proof of direct prophetic authorship.

This register can grow during research. Authenticity means that the material and its provenance are identifiable; it does not establish the truth of a historical or theological claim. The browser can show genuine traceable scans from institutional or non-institutional sources. Unknown mirrors remain leads until their provenance is established; reconstructions are labeled separately and generated images never count as manuscript evidence. A polemical article can be examined as an attributed argument, not substituted for the manuscript it discusses. Academic disagreements remain attributed; an official host does not make every historical interpretation certain.

The specific [primary-text and context-only policy](../plans/research-method-and-model-architecture.md) remains in force. Hadith is excluded by default; any direct-provenance exception requires separate evidence and can serve only as historical context. Tafsir and other commentary can be examined as documented later interpretations, without gaining authority over the primary text. A Quran manuscript source does not silently add its website's unrelated commentary or hadith to the evidence.
